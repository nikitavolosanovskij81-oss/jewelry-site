About the brand

Our story

Forged in Light began as a small workshop with one bench, a handful of tools and a simple idea: jewelry should be something you reach for every morning, not only on special occasions. We shared our first rings with friends, and the pieces travelled from hand to hand until a small community grew around them.

Today we still design every piece ourselves. Each ring, pair of earrings and necklace begins as a sketch, becomes a wax model and is finally cast, polished and checked by hand. We keep our collections small on purpose, so every design gets the attention it deserves.

Materials

We work only with precious metals and carefully selected stones:

  • Gold: 14K and 18K, with a warm tone that stays beautiful for decades.
  • Sterling silver: 925 standard, finished with a protective polish.
  • Diamonds and clear stones: chosen for brilliance and cut rather than size.

All metals come from certified suppliers, and we reuse offcuts from our own workshop whenever possible.

Philosophy

We believe in minimalism: clean lines, soft shapes and nothing that does not need to be there. Our jewelry is meant to work with the way you already dress and to quietly become part of your story.

Making less, but making it well, also means making it last. Every piece comes with a lifetime cleaning service and free resizing within the first year.
